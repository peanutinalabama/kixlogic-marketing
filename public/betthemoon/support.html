How to play

  1. You and your partner (the player across from you) play against the two players on either side. Empty seats are played by the computer.
  2. Each round, players bid the number of points their team will take, from 60 up to 120. The highest bid wins.
  3. The winning bidder picks up the 5-card kitty, puts 5 cards back, and names trump.
  4. Play 11 tricks. Follow the suit that was led if you can; the highest trump, or else the highest card of the suit led, takes the trick.
  5. Point cards: every 5 is worth 5, every 10 and 13 is worth 10, and the Comet is worth 20. The first team to 500 wins.

What is the Comet?

The Comet is Bet the Moon's special card. It always counts as trump and beats every other card.

What happens if my team misses its bid?

The bid is subtracted from your score. Bid boldly, but not too boldly.

Playing with friends online

  1. Tap Friends, enter your email and the 6-digit code we send you, and pick a screen name.
  2. Tap Create a table and share the 4-letter code. Your friends tap Friends, enter the code and tap Join.
  3. Everyone chooses a seat. Partners sit across from each other: North with South, East with West. The host picks how strong the computer players are and taps Start the game. Anyone not there yet is replaced by a computer player.
  4. If your connection drops, the app reconnects on its own and the computer plays for you after about 20 seconds. Come back and you pick up your seat. Tap Rejoin table if you closed the app.

Reporting and blocking a player

Players only see each other's screen names. If a name is offensive or pretends to be someone else, open the menu (or the lobby) and tap Report name next to it. If three different players report a name, it is removed automatically and that player must choose a new one. Tap Block to make sure you are never seated at a table with that player again. You can also email support@kixlogic.com and we will act within 24 hours.

Deleting my account

Tap Friends, then Delete my account. Your email, screen name, sessions, reports and blocks are deleted immediately.

Can I change how hard it is?

When playing alone, open the menu to choose the computer players' level (Beginner, Intermediate, or Expert), the game speed, and whether sound effects and music play. Online, the host chooses the level.

Does it work offline?

Playing alone works offline, always. Online play needs an internet connection.
