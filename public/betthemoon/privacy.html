Bet the Moon Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 2, 2026

Bet the Moon is published by Kixlogic LLC. Contact: support@kixlogic.com.

The short version: playing alone collects nothing at all: no account, no sign-in, no network connection. Online play with friends (added in version 1.1) is optional. If you use it, we keep your email address and a screen name so you can sign in and sit at a table, and nothing else about you.

Playing alone

  • No account, no login, no email or name collection.
  • Your settings (computer player level, game speed, sound and music) and the game in progress stay on your device only. We cannot see them.
  • No network requests are made while you play alone. The game works in airplane mode.

Playing online with friends (version 1.1 and later)

Online play needs an account. Here is everything we keep, and why:

  • Your email address. Used only to email you a 6-digit sign-in code and to recognise you when you come back. It is never shown to other players, never sold and never used for marketing. There are no passwords.
  • Your screen name. The one thing other players at your table can see. Please don't use your real name if you'd rather not.
  • Sign-in codes and sessions. We store only one-way scrambled copies, so a copy of our database cannot be used to sign in as you. Sign-in codes expire after 10 minutes, and the network address a code was requested from is kept for up to two days to stop abuse, then deleted automatically.
  • The table you are sitting at. Which seat you hold and the game in progress, kept on our server only while the table is open. Tables are deleted after at most six hours without activity, and finished games are not saved.
  • Reports and blocks. If you report or block another player, or someone reports you, we keep that record so we can look into it and keep blocked players apart.

Our servers run on Cloudflare, and Cloudflare Email Sending delivers the sign-in emails. They process this data for us and for no other purpose.

What Bet the Moon never does

  • No advertising, no analytics, no trackers, no third-party software that collects data about you.
  • No access to your contacts, photos, camera, microphone or location. The app requests no permissions.
  • No purchases. Bet the Moon contains no in-app purchases and no real-money transactions.
  • No chat. Players only ever see each other's screen names.
  • We do not sell your data or share it with anyone for their own use.

Bids are points, not money

A "bid" in Bet the Moon is the number of card points your team promises to take in a round. Nothing of value is wagered, won or lost. The game involves no gambling of any kind.

Deleting your data

Open Friends in the app and choose Delete my account. This immediately and permanently deletes your email address, screen name, sign-in sessions, reports and blocks. If you only played alone, deleting the app removes everything, because everything was on your device. You can also email support@kixlogic.com with any privacy request.

Children

Playing alone collects no personal information from anyone. Online play is for people aged 13 and over, and the app asks you to confirm your age before you pick a screen name. If you believe a child under 13 has an account, email us and we will delete it.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version appears on this page with a new effective date.
