Bet the Moon Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 1, 2026

Bet the Moon is published by Kixlogic LLC. Contact: support@kixlogic.com.

The short version: Bet the Moon collects nothing. There is no account, no sign-in, no analytics, and the app makes no network connections. Nothing you do in the game leaves your phone.

What Bet the Moon keeps

  • Your settings (computer player level, game speed, sound and music) and the game in progress. These are kept on your device only. They are never sent to Kixlogic, and we cannot see them.

That is the entire list.

What Bet the Moon does not do

  • No account, no login, no email or name collection.
  • No advertising, no analytics, no trackers, no third-party software development kits that collect data.
  • No network requests. The game plays fully in airplane mode.
  • No access to your contacts, photos, camera, microphone, or location. The app requests no permissions.
  • No purchases. Bet the Moon contains no in-app purchases and no real-money transactions.

Players at the table

Your partner and both opponents are computer-controlled players that run on your device. There is no online multiplayer, so you are never matched with, visible to, or able to message another person.

Bids are points, not money

A "bid" in Bet the Moon is the number of card points your team promises to take in a round. Nothing of value is wagered, won, or lost. The game involves no gambling of any kind.

Deleting your data

Deleting the app removes everything it saved, because everything it saved was on your device. We hold nothing on our servers. Email support@kixlogic.com with any privacy request.

Children

Bet the Moon does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children, because it collects no personal information at all.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version appears on this page with a new effective date.
