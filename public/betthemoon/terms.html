Bet the Moon Terms of Use

Effective date: November 2, 2026 · Kixlogic LLC

What Bet the Moon is

Bet the Moon is a partnership card game. You and a partner bid, name trump, and play tricks against two opponents, using Bet the Moon's own 49-card deck. You can play alone against computer players, or, from version 1.1, with friends at an online table. Seats nobody takes are played by the computer.

What Bet the Moon is not

Bet the Moon is not gambling. A bid is a number of card points, not a stake. There is no wagering, no entry fee, no prize, and no way to win or lose anything of value. There is no matchmaking with strangers and no chat: online tables are made by sharing a 4-letter code.

No purchases

Bet the Moon contains no in-app purchases and no subscriptions. If that ever changes, it will change in a future version with its own terms.

Accounts and screen names (online play)

  • Your screen name is shown to the other players at your table. It must not be offensive, must not pretend to be another person, the game or Kixlogic, and must not contain contact details.
  • You are responsible for what you do with your account. Don't share your sign-in code with anyone.
  • We may remove a screen name or an account that breaks these rules, including after reports from other players. You can report or block any player from inside the game.
  • You can delete your account at any time from the app (Friends, then Delete my account).
