What Bet the Moon is

Bet the Moon is a single-player partnership card game. You and a computer partner bid, name trump, and play tricks against two computer opponents, using Bet the Moon's own 49-card deck.

What Bet the Moon is not

Bet the Moon is not gambling. A bid is a number of card points, not a stake. There is no wagering, no entry fee, no prize, and no way to win or lose anything of value. Bet the Moon is not an online service and has no multiplayer.
