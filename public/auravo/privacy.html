Auravo Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 13, 2026

Auravo is published by Kixlogic. Contact: privacy@kixlogic.com.

What the app does

Auravo is a speaking-first language coach. It listens to your spoken practice, analyzes it, updates a private student model, and replies with spoken teaching.

Data we collect

  • Account profile you enter: name, optional email, languages, level, voice preference.
  • Learning records on this device: skill graph, session summaries, XP, badges, classroom profiles.
  • Purchase status from Apple (not your full card details).

Microphone and speech

The microphone is used only during a live lesson after you tap the record control. Speech recognition uses Apple’s Speech framework. Audio is processed in memory for that turn. Auravo does not store raw audio recordings by default. You may revoke access in iOS Settings.

What we do not collect

  • We do not sell personal data.
  • We do not use third-party advertising SDKs or track you across other companies’ apps.
  • We do not collect precise location or HealthKit data.

Children

Auravo is intended for ages 13 and up. It is not directed at children under 13 and is not a COPPA or K–12 classroom product in version 1.

Deletion

Settings → Delete my account removes the local SwiftData store. You may also delete the app. Email privacy@kixlogic.com for access or deletion requests.
