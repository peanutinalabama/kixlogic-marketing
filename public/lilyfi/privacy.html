LilyFi Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 20, 2026

LilyFi is published by Kixlogic LLC. Contact: support@kixlogic.com.

The short version: LilyFi never connects to your bank, never sees your accounts, and never sells or shares your information. What you tell Lily stays on your device and in your own iCloud.

What LilyFi stores

  • Your envelopes, transactions, and reminders — the numbers and names you enter yourself. They live on your device and, if you are signed in to iCloud, sync through your private iCloud database. Kixlogic has no server that stores your budget and no ability to read your iCloud data.
  • Your subscription status, managed by Apple through StoreKit. We never see your payment details.

Voice

When you talk to Lily, your speech is converted to text using Apple Speech Recognition, on your device whenever your device supports it. LilyFi never records or stores audio.

The coach service

When you use Lily's natural-language coaching (a paid feature), the text of what you said, your envelope names and current balances, and the last few lines of your conversation are sent over an encrypted connection to our coach service, which uses a large language model to work out what you meant. This data is used only to answer that turn. It is not stored by Kixlogic, not used to train models, and never includes your transaction history, name, email, location, or any account identifier. If you never subscribe, nothing ever leaves your device at all.

What LilyFi does not do

  • No bank or card connections. No screen scraping, no statements, no Plaid.
  • No account, no login, no email collection.
  • No advertising, no analytics SDKs, no trackers, no selling of data.

Deleting your data

Settings → Delete all data removes every envelope, transaction, and reminder from your device and your iCloud. Because we hold nothing on our servers, there is nothing else to delete. Email support@kixlogic.com for any privacy request.

Children

LilyFi is rated for ages 13 and up and does not knowingly collect any personal information from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version ships inside the app and on this page with a new effective date.
