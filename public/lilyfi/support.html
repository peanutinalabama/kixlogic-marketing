How to use LilyFi

  1. Pick your envelopes — every dollar gets a home.
  2. Tell Lily what you spent: type it, or tap the microphone and say it ("I spent $22 at Walmart").
  3. Lily confirms out loud; the app does the math. Say "undo" to reverse any change.

Microphone

Voice coaching needs the microphone and Apple speech recognition. Enable both in iOS Settings → Privacy if you declined the prompt. Audio is processed live and never saved.

No bank connection — on purpose

LilyFi never connects to your accounts. Balances reflect what you tell it, which is what keeps your data yours.

Subscriptions

LilyFi Plus is sold through Apple In-App Purchase with a 7-day free trial. Restore purchases from the paywall or Settings. Manage or cancel in iOS Settings → Apple Account → Subscriptions. The free plan keeps working forever.
