How to play

  1. Pick a mode: Trivia, Arcade, or Duel.
  2. Watch the ball machine. When a called number is on your card, that square glows.
  3. Tap the glowing square and beat the challenge — a question, a quick skill test, or a tap race against a rival.
  4. Win the square. Five in a row, column, or diagonal is BINGO.

Why can't I tap a square?

A square only becomes tappable after its number has been called. Squares that are dim are waiting on the ball machine; the glowing ones are yours to claim right now. If you just missed a challenge, that square rests briefly before you can try it again — you never lose it.

Chains and free fills

Claim squares back to back to build a chain. Longer chains pay more coins, and a three-chain earns a free fill you can spend on any square without a challenge.

Coins and the shop

Coins are earned by playing. They cannot be purchased — Bingopalooza contains no in-app purchases. Everything in the shop is cosmetic: card skins and dauber stamps that change how the game looks, never how it plays.

Are the opponents real people?

No. Every opponent is a computer-controlled character running on your phone. There is no online multiplayer and no way for anyone to contact you through the game.

Does it work offline?

Yes, always. Bingopalooza makes no network connections at all.

I lost my coins

Coins are stored on your device, so deleting and reinstalling the app clears them, and they do not move to a new phone. Email us if something looks wrong and we will help.
