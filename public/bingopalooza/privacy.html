Bingopalooza Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 26, 2026

Bingopalooza is published by Kixlogic LLC. Contact: support@kixlogic.com.

The short version: Bingopalooza collects nothing. There is no account, no sign-in, no analytics, and the app makes no network connections at all. Nothing you do in the game leaves your phone.

What Bingopalooza stores

  • Your coin balance and which cosmetic items you own and have equipped. This is saved on your device only. It is not backed up to any Kixlogic server, and we cannot see it.

That is the entire list.

What Bingopalooza does not do

  • No account, no login, no email or name collection.
  • No advertising, no analytics SDKs, no trackers, no third-party software development kits of any kind.
  • No network requests. The game is fully playable in airplane mode because it never contacts a server.
  • No access to your contacts, photos, camera, microphone, or location. The app requests no permissions.
  • No purchases. Bingopalooza contains no in-app purchases and no real-money transactions.

Opponents

The players you compete against are computer-controlled characters that run on your device. Bingopalooza has no online multiplayer, so you are never matched with, visible to, or able to message another person.

Coins

Coins are earned by playing and can only be spent on cosmetic items inside the game. They have no cash value, cannot be bought, cannot be sold, and cannot be transferred. Bingopalooza involves no wagering and no real-money gambling of any kind.

Deleting your data

Deleting the app removes everything it saved, because everything it saved was on your device. We hold nothing on our servers, so there is nothing else to delete. Email support@kixlogic.com with any privacy request.

Children

Bingopalooza does not knowingly collect any personal information from anyone, including children, because it collects no personal information at all.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version appears on this page with a new effective date.
