What Bingopalooza is

Bingopalooza is a single-player skill game built on a bingo board. Called numbers open squares; you win each square by answering a question, beating a short skill challenge, or out-tapping a computer-controlled rival.

What Bingopalooza is not

Bingopalooza is not gambling. There is no wagering, no entry fee, no prize, and no way to win or lose anything of value. Coins are earned by playing, exist only inside the app, have no cash value, and cannot be bought, sold, redeemed, or transferred. Bingopalooza is not an online service and has no multiplayer.

No purchases

Bingopalooza contains no in-app purchases and no subscriptions. The entire game is free. If that ever changes, it will change in a future version with its own terms, and nothing you already own will be taken away.

Your data
