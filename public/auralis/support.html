How to use Auralis

  1. Choose a mission on Today.
  2. Listen to your teacher, then tap the microphone and speak a full sentence.
  3. After the session, review the report and keep practicing.

Microphone

Auralis needs the microphone and Apple speech recognition to hear you. Enable both in iOS Settings → Privacy if you declined the prompt. Audio is not saved as a recording.

Subscriptions

Auralis enrollment is sold through Apple In-App Purchase. Restore purchases from Settings. Manage or cancel in iOS Settings → Apple Account → Subscriptions. Extra Lesson packs are consumable and are not restored.
