What Pixalary is

Pixalary is a single-player picture-guessing game. A hidden picture is uncovered tile by tile as you answer quick questions, and you win by naming it before a computer rival does.

Pixalary is not gambling. There is no wagering, no entry fee, no prize, and nothing of value to win or lose. It is not an online service and has no multiplayer.
