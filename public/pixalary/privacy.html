Pixalary Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 19, 2026

Pixalary is published by Kixlogic LLC for iPhone and Android. Contact: support@kixlogic.com.

The short version: Solo play collects nothing and never leaves your phone. If you choose to play online, our game server receives the display name you pick and a random player number so it can run the match. There is no account, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising and nothing to buy.

Solo play

Playing against Scout, Hunter or Oracle happens entirely on your device. It makes no network connections; all pictures and questions ship inside the app. The app saves your chosen rival, a short list of recently played pictures (so the game does not repeat itself) and the codes of any private groups you joined. These stay on your device and are not backed up to any Kixlogic server.

Online play

Online play (quick match, friend rooms and private groups) uses Kixlogic's game server, which runs on Cloudflare. When you use it, the server receives:

  • Your display name — whatever name you type. It is shown to the people you play and to members of groups you join. It does not need to be your real name.
  • A random player number — created by the app on your device the first time you play online. It is not linked to your phone's hardware, your Apple or Google account, or any other app.
  • Your moves in the match — answers, guesses and the specials you play, so the server can referee fairly.
  • Group details — if you create or join a private group: the group's name, its members' display names and player numbers, and any open challenges.

Match data is held in memory only while the match is running and is not kept afterwards. Group details are kept until you leave the group. Like any web server, Cloudflare processes your IP address to deliver the connection, and the server keeps short-lived request logs (deleted automatically within 7 days) that can include your IP address, display name and player number; we use them only to fix problems.

All online traffic is encrypted. There is no chat between players.

What Pixalary does not do

  • No account, no login, no email collection.
  • No advertising, no analytics SDKs, no trackers, no third-party SDKs that collect data.
  • No access to your contacts, photos, camera, microphone or location.
  • No purchases and no real-money transactions.
  • We never sell or share your data.

The pictures

Every picture in Pixalary is in the public domain or released under Creative Commons Zero. Each one records its licence and source, and none depicts a private individual.

Deleting your data

Tap Leave group to remove yourself from a group's member list. Deleting the app removes everything stored on your device. For any other request, email support@kixlogic.com and we will delete what the server holds for your player number.

Children

Pixalary is not directed at children under 13, and online play is intended for players 13 and over. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has played online, email us and we will delete their data.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version appears on this page with a new effective date.
