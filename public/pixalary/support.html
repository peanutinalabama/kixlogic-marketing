How to play

  1. Pick a rival — Scout, Hunter, or Oracle — and optionally a category.
  2. A picture hides under 100 tiles. Answer each five-second question correctly and a tile is removed.
  3. Get one wrong, or run out of time, and you wait five seconds while your rival keeps playing.
  4. Hit the ❗ button the moment you think you know the picture. First correct guess wins.

Blockers and jumpers

Some answers carry a surprise. A jumper reveals three tiles at once. A blocker makes your rival's next correct answer pay nothing — and your rival can play them on you too. The game always tells you when one lands.

Guessing

Close spellings count — one typo is forgiven on longer answers, and capitalisation and accents never matter. Wrong guesses cost 5 seconds, then 10, then 20, so guess when you know rather than spamming.

Is my rival a real person?

No. Every rival is a computer player running on your phone. It sees only its own board — never yours, and never the answer.

Does it work offline?

Yes, always. Pixalary makes no network connections at all.

Where do the pictures come from?

Every picture is public domain or Creative Commons Zero — famous artworks, animals, landmarks, and everyday things. Each records its source, and every one is genuinely guessable.
